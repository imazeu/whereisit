    Tap a word, then a row. Tap a placed word to lift it back off.

    How to play

    Six words sit in rows A to F, one per row. The clues never mention the word you're asked for, so place the others and find it by elimination. "Between" means anywhere between; "just above" means the row directly above. You get one answer. Board checks are counted in your result.

    Daily puzzles are the same for everyone that day. Share a Practice result and friends get your exact puzzle by its code.
