Where Is It?

Harder than you think. Use the clues, place the words, find the hidden one.

0:00

    Tap a word, then a row. Tap a placed word to pick it up. Tap a clue to cross it off.

    How to play

    Finished a Practice puzzle? Share it and your friends get your exact puzzle via its code.

    Six words sit in rows A to F, one per row. The clues never mention the word you're asked about, so you have to place everything else and find it by elimination. "Between" means somewhere between, not necessarily right next to. "Just above" means the row directly above. You get one go at the answer. "Check my grid" marks your placed words right or wrong, but it's counted against you.

    Daily puzzles are the same for everyone on the same date. Practice gives you endless new ones.
